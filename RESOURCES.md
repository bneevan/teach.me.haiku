## Knowledge

- [Book: _The Haiku Handbook: How to Write, Share, and Teach Haiku_ by William J. Higginson & Penny Harter](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/244365.The_Haiku_Handbook)
  The definitive foundational reference on history, aesthetics, and cross-cultural craft in English and Japanese. Use for: mechanics of syllable vs [*on*](./GLOSSARY.md#on), historical lineage from Bashō to modern free-form.
- [Book: _Traces of Dreams: Landscape, Cultural Memory, and the Poetry of Bashō_ by Haruo Shirane](https://www.sup.org/books/title/?id=1416)
  Scholarly masterwork on Bashō's balance of *fueki* (the unchanging) and *ryūkō* (the fluid/contemporary), plus how seasonal words ([*kigo*](./GLOSSARY.md#kigo)) link to cultural memory.
- [Book: _Haiku: An Anthology of Japanese Poems and Modern English Haiku_ by R.H. Blyth (4 Volumes)](https://terebess.hu/english/haiku/blyth.html)
  Classic English translations and Zen aesthetic commentary on the Edo masters (Bashō, Buson, Issa, Shiki). Use for: reading authentic translations and understanding seasonal resonance.
- [Essays: Haiku Society of America (HSA) Official Definitions & Archives](https://www.hsa-haiku.org)
  Standards, guidelines, and contemporary prize-winning work. Use for: understanding why modern English haiku often ranges from 10–14 syllables rather than rigid 17.
- [Journal: _Frogpond: The Journal of the Haiku Society of America_](https://www.hsa-haiku.org/frogpond/)
  The leading international journal of contemporary English haiku. Use for: modern state-of-the-art haiku models.
- [Book: _The Heart of Haiku_ by Jane Hirshfield](https://www.amazon.com/Heart-Haiku-Kindle-Single-ebook/dp/B0054U7VEC)
  Concise, lyrical investigation of Bashō's spiritual and technical genius in capturing transient moments.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [The Haiku Foundation (THF)](https://thehaikufoundation.org)
  Global non-profit community hosting archives, weekly Haikupedia prompts, and the "Troutswirl" blog. Use for: active community critique, prompt challenges, and audio archives.
- [r/haiku (Reddit)](https://reddit.com/r/haiku)
  Active community with community feedback. Use for: rapid practice and constructive peer critiques on line breaks and cutting words.
- [Haiku Society of America Regional Chapters](https://www.hsa-haiku.org/regions/regions.htm)
  Local in-person and Zoom ginko (haiku walks) and critiquing circles. Use for: real-time feedback on seasonal observations.
