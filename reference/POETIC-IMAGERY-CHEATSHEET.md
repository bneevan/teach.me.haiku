A catalog of natural phenomena, weather shifts, hours of the day, and everyday human objects paired with distilled, evocative one-liners designed to act as **Image A** (the anchor) or **Image B** across a structural cut ([*kireji*](../GLOSSARY.md#kireji)).

---

## 1. Weather & Atmospheric Phenomena

- **Morning Frost** *(Winter Kigo)*  
  *"A silver skin tightening over the quiet grass."*  
  *Sensory:* Cold touch, brittle crystalline stillness.

- **Sudden Downpour** *(Summer Kigo)*  
  *"The street turns to dark mirrors under drumming air."*  
  *Sensory:* Loud acoustic rush, sudden slickness.

- **Autumn Fog** *(Autumn Kigo)*  
  *"Distant trees dissolve into gray breath."*  
  *Sensory:* Vanishing horizon, damp chill, quiet enclosure.

- **Heat Shimmer** *(Summer Kigo)*  
  *"The highway breathing like liquid glass in the white sun."*  
  *Sensory:* Optical wavering, oppressive dry heat.

- **Freezing Rain** *(Winter Kigo)*  
  *"Twigs encased in glass, clicking in the dark."*  
  *Sensory:* Brittle acoustic chimes, immobilized nature.

- **Thunderhead** *(Summer Kigo)*  
  *"An anvil of purple slate bruising the afternoon sky."*  
  *Sensory:* Looming weight, ozone charge in the air.

## 2. Time of Day & Ephemerality

- **False Dawn** *(Early Morning)*  
  *"A thin blue slit cutting the throat of the night."*  
  *Sensory:* Incipient cold light, pre-birdsong stillness.

- **Noon Glare** *(Midday)*  
  *"White sun erasing every shadow into chalk."*  
  *Sensory:* Bleached color, harsh vertical heat.

- **The Blue Hour / Dusk** *(Evening)*  
  *"The sky holding blue water just before spilling into night."*  
  *Sensory:* Melancholic transition, [*mono no aware*](../GLOSSARY.md#mono-no-aware).

- **Midnight Silence** *(Deep Night)*  
  *"A clock ticking only when nobody breathes."*  
  *Sensory:* Solitude, heightened auditory sensitivity.

## 3. Common Objects & Habitation

- **Rusty Gate Latch**  
  *"Cold iron that complains when the wind leans in."*  
  *Sensory:* Metallic grit, high-pitched screech, resistance to time.

- **Lukewarm Tea**  
  *"A pale ring on wood where warmth used to be."*  
  *Sensory:* Fading thermal energy, solitary reflection.

- **Ticking Car Engine**  
  *"Hot metal contracting slowly into the November dark."*  
  *Sensory:* Rhythmic metallic pings, quiet descent into cold.

- **Wet Umbrella**  
  *"Black silk dripping into a puddle on the porch floor."*  
  *Sensory:* Smell of wet nylon, steady drops on wood.

- **Forgotten Coat on Peg**  
  *"Shoulders of heavy wool holding the shape of someone who left."*  
  *Sensory:* Absent human presence, dusty fabric.

- **Cracked Ceramic Bowl**  
  *"A seam of gold repair where rough hands touched the clay."*  
  *Sensory:* Uneven texture, enduring imperfect beauty ([*wabi-sabi*](../GLOSSARY.md#wabi-sabi)).

- **Clothesline in Wind**  
  *"Empty linen billowing like ghosts learning how to run."*  
  *Sensory:* Snapping canvas, clean fresh scent.

## 4. Flora & Botanical Life

- **Cherry Blossom Drift** *(Spring Kigo)*  
  *"A confetti of pale light falling faster than snow."*  
  *Sensory:* Weightless swirl, transient perfection.

- **Fallen Persimmon** *(Autumn Kigo)*  
  *"An orange sun bruised open against wet gravel."*  
  *Sensory:* Pungent sweet scent, autumn decay.

- **Bare Winter Oak** *(Winter Kigo)*  
  *"Black iron veins hammered into the gray sky."*  
  *Sensory:* Stripped austerity, structural silhouette.

- **Pine Needles on Rock**  
  *"A copper weave muffling the footsteps of the rain."*  
  *Sensory:* Resinous aroma, cushioned acoustic footsteps.

- **Dry Creek Bed** *(Summer)*  
  *"Smooth white river stones waiting for the sky to break."*  
  *Sensory:* Chalky touch, thirst, expectant quiet.

- **Damp Tree Moss**  
  *"A green velvet coat softening the scars of the bark."*  
  *Sensory:* Cool sponge, damp earthy fragrance.

## 5. Fauna & Small Creatures

- **Garden Snail** *(Summer Kigo)*  
  *"A silver trail carrying its house toward the mountain."*  
  *Sensory:* Glistening moisture, humble persistence.

- **Crow on Wire** *(Winter Kigo)*  
  *"A single black knot tied against the sunset."*  
  *Sensory:* Minimalist punctuation mark, harsh caw.

- **Moth at Window** *(Summer)*  
  *"Dusty wings beating against the glass between heat and night."*  
  *Sensory:* Faint papery vibration, desperate longing.

- **Empty Cicada Husk** *(Late Summer Kigo)*  
  *"An amber ghost clinging to the pine, hollowed out by song."*  
  *Sensory:* Translucent brittle husk, phantom sound.

- **Firefly Pulse** *(Summer Kigo)*  
  *"A cold green spark drifting through dark corn, gone before you look."*  
  *Sensory:* Fleeting phosphorescence, warm summer gloom.

- **Fish Breaking Water** *(Spring / Summer)*  
  *"A silver needle stitching the pond surface to the evening air."*  
  *Sensory:* Instantaneous ripple, concentric circles breaking reflections.

## How to Construct Haiku from This Catalog

1. **Pick One Anchor (Image A):** e.g., *engine ticking down into the November dark*
2. **Add The Cut ([*Kireji*](../GLOSSARY.md#kireji)):** `—`
3. **Bring in a Contrasting Action (Image B):** e.g., *a single yellow leaf strikes the windshield*
4. **Result:**
   > *engine ticking down &mdash;*  
   > *a single yellow leaf strikes*  
   > *the cold windshield*
