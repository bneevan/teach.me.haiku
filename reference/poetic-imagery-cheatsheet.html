Poetic Imagery & Sensory Anchor Cheatsheet

Common objects, weather events, hours of time, and seasonal markers with distilled, ready-to-cut poetic one-liners. Also in markdown: POETIC-IMAGERY-CHEATSHEET.md.

How to Use This Cheatsheet for Composition:

Select any sensory anchor below as your Image A (the context). Place a cut (kireji: — or :) immediately after it. Then observe or imagine a sudden, contrasting action as your Image B. Do not explain the gap (yohaku)—let the poetry spark between them.

Morning Frost Winter Kigo
“A silver skin tightening over the quiet grass.”
Sensory: Cold touch, crystalline silence
Sudden Downpour Summer Kigo
“The street turns to dark mirrors under drumming air.”
Sensory: Heavy auditory rhythm, sudden slickness
