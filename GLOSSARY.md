The canonical language and tight definitions for haiku poetics, mechanics, and aesthetics in this workspace.

## Structural Mechanics

<a id="on"></a>
**On (音 / Onji)**:
A Japanese phonetic time unit (mora) that measures acoustic duration. A single English syllable typically contains 1.5 to 2 Japanese *on*.
_Avoid_: Syllables, beats, word count.

<a id="kireji"></a>
**Kireji (切れ字)**:
A structural "cutting word" (or in English, a dash, colon, ellipsis, or line break) that bisects the poem into two distinct syntactic and perceptual planes.
_Avoid_: Punctuation mark, pause button.

<a id="kigo"></a>
**Kigo (季語)**:
A designated seasonal word or phrase that anchors the poem in an ecological calendar and invokes shared cultural memory.
_Avoid_: Weather word, nature tag.

<a id="saijiki"></a>
**Saijiki (歳時記)**:
An organized compendium or almanac of recognized [*kigo*](#kigo), indexed by the four traditional seasons plus the New Year.
_Avoid_: Haiku dictionary, rhyming dictionary.

<a id="teikei"></a>
**Teikei (定型)**:
The traditional fixed formal pattern of 5-7-5 phonetic units ([*on*](#on)).
_Avoid_: Haiku meter, rigid rule.

## Compositional Strategies

<a id="toriawase"></a>
**Toriawase (取り合わせ)**:
The juxtaposition of two disparate, independent sensory images that create a cognitive spark or third unwritten meaning across the [*kireji*](#kireji).
_Avoid_: Contrast, collage, comparison.

<a id="ichibutsu-jitate"></a>
**Ichibutsu-jitate (一物仕立て)**:
A "one-thing composition" focusing entirely on a single subject or sensory event without an overt juxtaposition.
_Avoid_: Single-topic poem, flat description.

<a id="yohaku"></a>
**Yohaku (余白)**:
The pregnant "empty white space" or unspoken resonance left behind for the reader's imagination to complete.
_Avoid_: Blank space, vagueness, ambiguity.

<a id="shasei"></a>
**Shasei (写生)**:
Sketching directly from life; objective observation of empirical sensory phenomena without emotional editorializing or poetic embellishment.
_Avoid_: Realistic writing, nature journaling.

## Aesthetic Sensibilities

<a id="mono-no-aware"></a>
**Mono no aware (物の哀れ)**:
A poignant, gentle awareness of the transience and impermanence of all worldly things.
_Avoid_: Sadness, depression, nostalgia.

<a id="wabi-sabi"></a>
**Wabi-sabi (侘寂)**:
An aesthetic celebrating rustic simplicity, quiet austerity, imperfection, and the natural patina of time.
_Avoid_: Vintage vibe, rustic chic.

<a id="karumi"></a>
**Karumi (軽み)**:
A quality of lightness, casualness, and unforced ease championed by Bashō in his late years.
_Avoid_: Triviality, silliness, fluff.

<a id="senryu"></a>
**Senryu (川柳)**:
A 3-line poetic form identical in structure to haiku, but centered on human foibles, irony, humor, and psychological absurdity rather than nature or season.
_Avoid_: Funny haiku, satirical poem.
