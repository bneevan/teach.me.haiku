Mission: Mastering Haiku Composition & Appreciation

The concrete real-world anchor for this learning workspace. Plain markdown version: MISSION.md.

Why We Are Learning This

Develop the instinctive creative ability to capture brief, resonant human and natural moments in precise 3-line verse, moving fluently between rapid spontaneous snapshots and layered, contemplative poetry that evokes profound emotional resonance in the reader.

Success Looks Like (Observable Milestones)

✓ Rapid Snapshot: Write spontaneous "instant" haiku in under 60 seconds using the 3-beat sensory capture technique without artificial filler words.
✓ Deep Resonance: Compose contemplative haiku utilizing toriawase (juxtaposition) and the structural cut (kireji).
✓ Phonetic Precision: Accurately distinguish Japanese on (morae) from English syllables to eliminate the amateur 5-7-5 syllable bloat.
✓ Seasonal Depth: Identify and deploy kigo (seasonal anchors) to evoke cultural memory without spelling it out.
✓ Genre Clarity: Instantly distinguish true haiku from senryu (human foibles/humor) and abstract pseudo-haiku.
○ Empirical Shasei & Wabi-Sabi: Sketch empirical sensory facts without editorial adjectives (shasei) and compose single-object poems (ichibutsu-jitate).

Constraints

  • Modern English haiku conventions (focusing on brevity, sensory immediacy, and 10–14 syllable cadence) prioritized alongside classical roots (5-7-5 onji).
  • Dual-mode delivery: Eyes-free audio narration paired with ears-free visual reference.

Out of Scope

  • Full renga / renku linked-verse multi-author composition parties.
  • Classical Edo-period Japanese archaic grammar rules beyond foundational terms.
